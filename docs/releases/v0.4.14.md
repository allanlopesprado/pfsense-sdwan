Under **Services → SD-WAN → Diagnostics**, the **Results** panel shows the recommended member, the reason for the recommendation, SLA limits, and the number of eligible members.

The table maps each member to its gateway, separates the policy decision from the link status, and shows RTT, RTTsd, loss, and cost for cost-based policies. It highlights measurements above the limits. When the service is stopped or a current sample is unavailable, the page explains why there is no evaluation.
